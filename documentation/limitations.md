# Limitations

## Study-design limitations

- RTLS adoption, implementation scope, vendor, timing, and maturity are unobserved. RVPI cannot estimate causal RTLS effects.
- Trust-level aggregate indicators cannot identify ward-, department-, asset-, pathway-, staff-, or patient-level mechanisms.
- The design does not contain implementation costs, cash-releasing savings, workflow suitability, technology readiness, or return-on-investment data.
- High-ranked trusts are candidates for further local diagnosis, not poor performers or confirmed investment targets.

## Data limitations

- ERIC 2024–25 lags the 2025–26 activity sources by one year.
- Publication revisions, changing provider boundaries, mergers, and reporting-scope differences may affect comparability.
- Missing values are not imputed; a score requires at least four of six components.
- Trust-level denominators may hide variation across sites and services.
- Official workbook and CSV schemas may change. The cleaners deliberately fail rather than guessing when expected fields are absent.

## Index limitations

- Equal weighting is transparent but normative; it is not evidence that all components contribute equally to RTLS value potential.
- Several components capture related acute-flow pressures, creating possible conceptual overlap or partial double counting.
- Z-scores are sample dependent and sensitive to the selected provider population and outliers.
- Primary and full-provider scores are not directly interchangeable because they are standardised separately.

## Clustering limitations

K-means clusters are exploratory. Cluster counts and assignments were not stable between the primary and full-provider samples. Clusters must not be treated as validated intervention groups, causal effect segments, or ROI categories.

## Appropriate use

RVPI should be used to prioritise local workflow mapping, asset-utilisation review, staff time-motion studies, implementation-feasibility assessment, and prospective evaluation. It should not be used as a procurement rule or performance league table.
